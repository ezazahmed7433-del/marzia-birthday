Dear Marzia,

First of all, happy 15th birthday. And yes, I know this birthday gift arrived late. Exams decided to destroy the timing, so technically this website is also a victim of the education system.

I could have simply wished you happy birthday, sent a message and disappeared. But that felt way too ordinary.

So I made this little corner of the internet instead. It may not be perfect, but every part of it exists because I wanted to make something that you could actually remember.

We've talked about a lot of things. Life, problems, the future, random nonsense, serious conversations that somehow become jokes, and those conversations that continue for hours without either of us realizing how much time passed.

And honestly, that's one of the things I appreciate about our friendship.

Life isn't always going to be easy. There will be confusing days, frustrating days, days where nothing seems to make sense. But I hope you never forget that life isn't only about surviving the difficult parts.

You have to notice the small things too. The stupid jokes. The random conversations. The people who make an ordinary day slightly less ordinary. The memories that seem meaningless at first but become precious later.

So don't spend your whole life waiting for everything to become perfect before you allow yourself to enjoy it.

Breathe. Laugh. Make mistakes. Learn. Grow. And most importantly, live.

A difficult chapter is not the whole book.

There are still so many pages ahead of you, and I genuinely hope those pages contain good people, good memories, unexpected opportunities, and a lot of reasons to smile.

And whenever life gets unnecessarily complicated, remember that somewhere there is at least one idiot who will still be around to listen to your problems, argue with you, and probably make the situation even more confusing.

That's what friends are for.

So, Marzia, keep being yourself. Keep learning. Keep questioning things. Keep dreaming about the future. And don't lose the parts of yourself that make you uniquely you.

I hope 15 treats you well. And I hope when you look back at this birthday years from now, you'll remember that someone actually went through the trouble of making an entire ridiculous website for you.

Take care. Stay happy. Bachte shekho. Bhalo theko.

And whatever happens, I'll always be somewhere in your corner cheering for you.

— your Ezu
